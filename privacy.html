Privacy Policy

DOT RUSH is a simple hyper-casual game built for fun and enjoyment. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

DOT RUSH does not require you to sign up or provide any personal information to play. The game may collect anonymous usage statistics (such as your score, session length, and number of plays) to help us understand player engagement and improve gameplay.

Advertising

DOT RUSH displays ads to support development costs. These ads are provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) which may collect data according to their own privacy policies. We do not have access to or store this information.

Cookies and Local Storage

The game uses your browser’s local storage to save settings, coins, best scores, and cosmetics you’ve unlocked. This information never leaves your device and is only used to enhance your experience.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or the game, please feel free to contact the developer via GitHub.
